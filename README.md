# Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does
not exist — for [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
`u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e`.

> In Gloamwick, showers till nine, then sunshine — the bus is late, the coffee is hot, and the sky just apologised in gold.

121 characters (limit 140). `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

Written against the judge brief, most important first:

1. **A forecast for a city that does not exist** — Gloamwick is named, and
   its weather (showers till nine, then sunshine) is the whole point.
2. **Makes a bored commuter smile on one reading** — the bus is late
   (recognition), the coffee is hot (small comfort), then the turn: the sky
   apologised in gold, a surprising image that reframes the rotten morning
   as forgiven.
3. **Keeps the register of a real forecast** — "showers till nine, then
   sunshine" is the rhythm of broadcast weather, bent rather than dropped.
4. **One concrete detail of the city's life** — the late bus and the hot
   coffee make Gloamwick feel like a place with commuters in it.
5. **Every word earns its place** — 121 characters, well inside the cap.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
